How do I change my tasks?

Open the You tab and choose Daily tasks. You can have up to four.

What is strict mode?

With strict mode on, missing a day restarts the challenge at day one. It is part of Forge 75 Pro and can be turned off at any time in the You tab.

How do I restart?

You tab, then Restart challenge. This clears your progress and starts at day one today.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Forge 75. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

You tab, then Restore purchases.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
