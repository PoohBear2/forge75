Privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Summary

Forge 75 does not collect any data about you. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your name, goal, tasks, daily progress, routine, signature, and any proof or progress photos you add are saved only in the app's storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising or tracking software in the app.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera or your photo library only when you choose to add a photo. Photos stay on your device.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

Forge 75 Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
